HOW THE ESTIMATE WORKS

One model, fitted to this snapshot

MODEL radar-2

Implied value = TVL × category base multiple × growth × maturity × chain breadth. The base multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio among live tokens in that category when at least five samples exist; otherwise it uses the snapshot-wide live-token median. Multiples are bounded from 0.05× to 2.00×.

Growth1 + weekly change clamped to −60%…120%, weighted by 0.5
Maturity0.6 + age scaled to 1,095 days × 0.4
Breadth3% per additional chain, up to four
